# Forensic Security Intelligence (Trí tuệ Pháp y Bảo mật)

> **"Identity is not enough; intent is everything."**

Forensic Security Intelligence is the elite capability of an Agent to deconstruct, analyze, and audit any file for malicious intent before processing. In the 2026 era of agentic workflows, this skill acts as the "Air Gap" between untrusted external data and the internal system.

## Command Center
- **Core Mission**: Detect stolen secrets, malware heuristics, and structural anomalies.
- **Forensic Engine**: `scripts/audit_engine.py`
- **Current Status**: v1.0.0 (Elite 2026 Standard)

## Security Verdict Protocol
- **CLEAN**: Safe for processing.
- **SUSPICIOUS**: Potential risk, proceed with caution/sandbox.
- **DANGEROUS**: High risk detected (Malware/Keys). STOP and Report.
