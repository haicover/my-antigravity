# Product Discovery & Research (Khám phá & Nghiên cứu) — Elite 2026

Product Discovery is the process of reducing uncertainty. In 2026, we use a hybrid of human empathy and AI velocity to find "Product-Market Fit" faster than ever.

---

## 🔍 1. Modern Market & Competitor Analysis
Gone are the days of manual spreadsheets.
- **Autonomous Competitive Intelligence**: Setting up agents to track competitor changelogs, pricing shifts, and API updates.
- **Trend Spotting**: Using NLP on social platforms to identify emerging user "pains" before they hit mainstream reports.
- **TAM/SAM/SOM 2.0**: Using dynamic data providers to estimate market size in real-time.

## 👥 2. Deep User Research (Qualitative)
- **Continuous Interviewing**: Weekly slots with real users to test assumptions.
- **AI-Augmented Synthesis**: Recording interviews and using LLMs to tag key themes (Pain, Gain, Motivation).
- **Empathy Mapping**: Visualizing what users say, do, think, and feel.

## 💡 3. Idea Generation & Prioritization
- **Double Diamond Model**: Discover -> Define -> Develop -> Deliver.
- **Validation Boards**: Tracking "Assumptions" and how they were tested (e.g., Landing page tests, Concierge MVPs).
- **Feature vs. Outcome**: Always asking "What behavior change are we driving?" instead of "What button are we adding?"

---

## 🛠️ Tools of the Trade
- **Research**: Dovetail, Maze, Otter.ai.
- **Intelligence**: Perplexity (for deep scanning), SensorTower.
- **Design**: Figma (for rapid prototyping).

> [!TIP]
> **Discovery-Led Growth**: Don't trust what users *say* they want. Trust what they *do*. Always look at behavioral data (Retention) over survey data.
