# Product Strategy & Growth (Chiến lược & Tăng trưởng) — Elite 2026

Strategy is about making choices. Growth is about scaling the value of those choices. In 2026, we focus on **Product-Led Growth (PLG)** and **Data-Obsessed Strategy**.

---

## 🎯 1. High-Precision Product Strategy
- **Positioning**: Where do we play? How do we win?
- **The Moat**: What makes our product hard to replicate? (Network effects, Data, Brand, System of Record).
- **Vision vs. Tactics**: Maintaining a 2-year North Star while staying flexible in 2-week execution chunks.

## 📈 2. Modern Product Metrics (AARRR 2.0)
We move beyond vanity metrics to "Retention-First" growth.
- **Acquisition**: Efficiency over volume (LTV > CAC).
- **Activation**: The "Aha! Moment" — When does the user get the core value?
- **Retention**: The ultimate truth. If users don't come back, your product is broken.
- **Referral**: Designing viral loops into the product DNA.
- **Revenue**: Monetizing value delivered, not just access.

## 🚀 3. Product-Led Growth (PLG)
The product *is* the salesperson.
- **Self-Serve**: Can a user solve their problem without talking to a human?
- **Virality**: Does using the product invite more users? (e.g., Slack, DocuSign).
- **Expansion**: Identifying signals that a user needs a higher tier/more seats.

---

## 💡 Expert Metric: The "North Star"
Your North Star Metric should represent the **Value** delivered to the user, not just business profit. For YOCHECKIN, it might be: *"Number of patients successfully checked in without manual data entry."*
